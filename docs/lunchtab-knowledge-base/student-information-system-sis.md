## Page 1

Lunchtab supports integration with student information systems (SIS) to facilitate the automatic import of student and family account data. Data is synchronized on a daily basis to ensure that the information in Lunchtab is always up-to-date. Currently supported student information systems are Blackbaud and Veracross . Steps for conﬁguring these services diﬀer slightly, so please refer to the section for your chosen SIS. If your school does not use one of these systems, it is still possible to manage and import your user data manually through the Lunchtab admin portal. Once your users have been imported, you will want to initiate the onboarding process for them to create their login credentials and activate their family accounts. For further details on managing users, please refer to the Users documentation. Before conﬁguring Blackbaud as your SIS, you will need to connect Lunchtab to your environment within the Blackbaud marketplace. Details on connecting applications to your Blackbaud environment can be found in the Blackbaud documentation. To conﬁgure Blackbaud as your SIS, access the SIS Integration view and select Blackbaud from the dropdown list. You'll now be required to authorize Lunchtab to access your Blackbaud account. Press Authenticate to begin the process. Sign in with your Blackbaud credentials and press Authorize to grant Lunchtab access, you will then be redirected back to the Lunchtab SIS Integration view. By default, the SIS import process will run once a day to synchronize data between the SIS and Lunchtab. You can use the Job panel to Conﬁgure the job to run at a diﬀerent time of day, Pause the job from running on a scheduled basis, or Run Now to immediately trigger the im- port process regardless of the daily schedule. To view detailed information on the most recent execution of the job, press View Detail. Once the SIS import process has completed and your users have been added to Lunchtab, you can run this job to add any photos held in the SIS as their proﬁle picture. This job is triggered manually and not run on a scheduled basis. Press Run Now to im- mediately trigger the import process and View Detail to see status of the most recent run.
