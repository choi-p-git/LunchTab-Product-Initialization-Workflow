## Page 1

Sales business types are the core of the Lunchtab service, and represent each of the diﬀerent stores or departments where you process your sales. Under each sales business type you'll manage your products, orders, menus, subscrip- tions and conﬁgure any related views within your point of sale. There are two ordering models for sales business types. The Cafeteria business type represents a cafeteria or restaurant model where orders are typically placed against a menu of meals. Administrators can publish a menu of meals for each day of the week, and customers can place orders in advance through the web portal. The current day's menus are also displayed as a page within the point of sale. The Store business type can be used to represent an online store with a catalog of products available for order. This may also be suitable for food and drink outlets where daily menus are not re- quired. Your Lunchtab school has been preconﬁgured with a Cafeteria business type, but you can easily create additional business types to represent other stores or departments. Sales business types can be created in the Sales Business Types view. To add a new business type, select Add Sales Business Type from the actions menu. Value Description Name A name for the sales business type. Business Type The ordering model (see Business Type). Display Sequence Optionally speciﬁes the order in which the sales business types will be displayed in the UI. Enable Online Ordering Whether customers can place orders through the website. If disabled, purchases may only be made at the point of sale. Published Whether the sales business type is visible to users in the customer portal. The sales business type can be hidden while it is initially being conﬁgured and published when ready to accept orders. Once the sales business type has been created, a new submenu will appear in the side- bar of the admin portal with links to the conﬁguration views for all the data related to the business.
