## Page 1

Lunchtab supports integration with payment gateways to allow users to add funds to their account through the website. Currently supported payment gateways are Stripe , Blackbaud Merchant Services , and Authorize.net . Steps for conﬁguring these services diﬀer slightly, so please refer to the section for your chosen payment provider. To conﬁgure Stripe as your payment provider, access the Payment Gateway view and select Stripe from the dropdown list. You'll be presented with the conﬁguration for Stripe. ## Page 4

To conﬁgure Blackbaud Merchant Services as your payment provider, access the Payment Gateway view and select Blackbaud from the dropdown list. You'll now be required to authorize Lunchtab to access your BBMS account. Press Authenticate to begin the process. Sign in with your Blackbaud credentials and press Authorize to grant Lunchtab access, you will then be redirected back to the Lunchtab payment gateway conﬁguration view. Select the BBMS payment conﬁguration that you wish to use for Lunchtab transactions from the dropdown list. Payment Conﬁguration To allow your users to make payments using their saved payment methods within Lunchtab, you must ensure that the selected payment conﬁguration is set to not apply AVS (Address Veriﬁcation Service) or CSC (Card Security Code) checks. You must use a payment conﬁguration where both of these values are set to 'None' or these transactions will be declined. For further information on conﬁguring these settings, please refer to the BBMS AVS and CSC Settings documentation. AVS and CSC Conﬁguration
