To send email notiﬁcations to users on your behalf we'll need you to provide a sender address and validate your domain by adding the required DNS records to your do- main. Access the Notiﬁcation Settings page and enter the address that should be used as the sender for email notiﬁcations. The process for adding the required DNS records will vary depending on your domain registrar. You may need to consult their documentation or ask for support from your IT department. After submitting the sender address, you'll be presented with an outline of the records that will need to be added to your domain. Copy the names and values of the DNS records and add them to your domain's DNS conﬁguration. Once you have added the records, press the Validate Domain button. Notiﬁcation Settings

If the records are found, your domain will display as veriﬁed and Lunchtab will be able to send email notiﬁcations from the sender address. If you need to change the sender address, you can do so at any time by returning to the Email Conﬁguration page and submitting the updated address. Note that the re- quired DNS settings will change and you will need to perform the validation process again. The sending of notiﬁcations can be temporarily disabled by checking the Disable Notiﬁcations Delivery checkbox. This can be useful when performing some bulk opera- tions that may generate a large number of undesired notiﬁcations. If you have added the records to your domain and the validation response indicates that the records are not found, return a few hours later and retry validation.
