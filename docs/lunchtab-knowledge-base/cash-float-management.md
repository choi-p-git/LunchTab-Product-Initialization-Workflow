Lunchtab supports the use of cash drawers connected to the POS terminal for accept- ing cash payments and providing change. To enable the cash drawer functionality, access the Settings menu from the main POS screen and navigate to the Cash Float tab. Here you can conﬁgure the cash drawer and track cash ﬂoat entering and leaving the drawer. Lunchtab supports cash drawer trigger devices that can be connected to the POS ter- minal and trigger the cash drawer to open automatically when a cash payment is pro- cessed. Most triggers that connect to the POS via USB and signal the drawer via a virtual serial port should be compatible. Feel free to contact us if you need assistance selecting a compatible cash drawer trig- ger device. To begin a cash ﬂoat session, you will perform a cash adjustment and enter the amount of cash ﬂoat being added to the drawer. On the Cash Float tab, press the Adjust Cash Float button. A dialog will appear prompting you to enter the ﬂoat adjustment amount. Enter the amount and press the Deposit button to record the cash entering the drawer. When partial ﬂoat amounts are removed from the drawer (for example, when removing a note to obtain change), the process is similar. Open the adjustment dialog and enter the amount being removed, then press the Withdraw button to record the cash leaving the drawer. in the drawer. To close the session, navigate to the Cash Float settings tab and press the Close Float Session button. The close ﬂoat dialog will show the amount of cash expected in the drawer based on recorded transactions and ﬂoat adjustments. Count the actual cash in the drawer and enter this amount into the dialog. Press the Conﬁrm button to proceed. The conﬁrmation dialog will summarize any discrepancy between the expected and actual cash amounts. If a receipt printer is connected, you can choose to print a sum- mary of the ﬂoat session for your records. Optionally add a note regarding the ﬂoat session before pressing the Close Session button to end the session. At the start of your next session, start the process again by performing a cash adjust- ment to add the new ﬂoat to the drawer. The Cash Float Report provides a summary of cash ﬂoat activity for a vending outlet over a speciﬁed date range. Select the desired date range and vending outlet to generate a report of all cash enter- ing and leaving the cash drawer during this period, along with any discrepancies noted during ﬂoat session closures.
