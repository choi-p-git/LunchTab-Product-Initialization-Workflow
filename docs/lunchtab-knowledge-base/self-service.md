## Page 1

Self-service modes allow consumers to process purchases without the immediate at- tendance of a cashier using a restricted interface. The conﬁgurations for self-service modes are managed under each sales business type. For further details on creating these conﬁgurations, see the POS Self Service documen- tation within the sales business types section. When a self-service conﬁguration is available, a Self Service button will appear in the left navigation menu of the cashier UI. Pressing this button will either present a list of available self-service conﬁgurations to choose from, or if only one conﬁguration is available it will be activated immediately. The POS application will switch to full-screen mode and present the interface deﬁned by the self-service conﬁguration. When a self-service conﬁguration with Check In mode is active, the vending outlet will await the presentation of customer credentials and immediately attempt to purchase a predeﬁned product for any identiﬁed user. Customers will be prompted to present their registered RFID / Fingerprint / Barcode to sign in. Activating Self Service Check In Mode Self Service

## Page 2

Upon successful authentication, the predeﬁned product will be purchased immediately using the customer's default family account balance. Conﬁrmation will be shown if the purchase is successful, with an option to print a receipt if printing is enabled. An error message will be displayed if the purchase is unsuccessful due to restrictions or insuﬃcient account balance. To override any restrictions a cashier will need to exit the self-service mode and process a sale through the normal cashier interface. Once the process has completed, the screen will return to the prompt for the next con- sumer to check in.

## Page 3

When a self-service conﬁguration with Self Service mode is active, a simpliﬁed version of the normal cashier experience will be presented while removing access to any ad- ministrative functions. Customers will initially be prompted to present their registered RFID / Fingerprint / Barcode to sign in. Upon successful authentication, the user will be able to navigate the available POS pages and add products to their basket using the same process as a cashier. Self Service Mode

## Page 4

Once the customer has added the desired items to their basket, they can complete the purchase by pressing the Complete Checkout button. If the purchase is successful, conﬁrmation will be shown with an option to print a receipt if printing is enabled. If checkout is unable to be completed due to restrictions or insuﬃcient account balance, an error message will be displayed and checkout will be prevented. A cashier will need to exit the self-service mode and continue the sale through the nor- mal cashier interface to override any restrictions and complete the sale.

Once the process has completed, the screen will return to the prompt for the next con- sumer to check in. When a self-service conﬁguration with Balance Check mode is active, the vending out- let will await the presentation of customer credentials and display the current balance of the identiﬁed user's Lunchtab family accounts. Customers will be prompted to present their registered RFID / Fingerprint / Barcode to sign in. Upon successful authentication, the balance of the user's associated family accounts will be displayed.
