Users with the necessary roles can log in to the Lunchtab POS application using their account email address and PIN. To conﬁgure a cashier PIN, access Account Settings and open the Cashier tab. Cashiers can also log in by presenting their registered ﬁngerprints or RFID card to a reader attached to the POS. For further information on registering ﬁngerprints and RFID cards, see Consumer Management. For a user to log in to the Lunchtab POS application, their account must have the Cashier role as a minimum. For further details on roles and permissions, see User Roles.
