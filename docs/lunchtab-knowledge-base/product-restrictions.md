Product restrictions allow speciﬁc products or categories to be marked as unsuitable for individuals or groups of users. Restrictions can represent unsuitability due to allergies, age restrictions, or any other reason that a product may be unsuitable for an individual or group. Users will be warned when they attempt to order a restricted product, and cashiers will be notiﬁed at the point of sale and required to conﬁrm an override of a restriction to al- low checkout to continue. Product restrictions for user groups are managed in the Product Restrictions view. To add a new product restriction, select Add Product Restriction from the actions menu. Value Description Target Users The type of user grouping the restriction will be applied to. Classiﬁcation / Group / Classroom / Campus / Boarding Type The subject group of the restriction (where the restriction is not applicable to all users). Restriction Type The type of content that is being restricted, either an individual product or category of products. Restriction Category / Base Product The selected content for the restriction. Product restrictions for individual users are collected during the onboarding process and can be managed by family administrators in the My Family view of the consumer portal. System administrators can conﬁgure restrictions for individual users by locating the Adding Restrictions For User Groups Individual User Restrictions Product Restrictions
