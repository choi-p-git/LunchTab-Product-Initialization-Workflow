## Page 1

Tax categories are used to apply sales tax to the purchase of products and subscrip- tions. When a tax category has been associated with an item, the tax rate will be applied to any purchases and summarized during web or point of sale checkout. Products or subscriptions may be associated with multiple tax categories to apply diﬀ- ﬀerent rates of tax (for example, 'state sales tax' and 'local sales tax'). Tax categories are managed in the Tax Categories view. To add a restriction category, select Add Tax Category from the actions menu. Value Description Name A name for the category. Tax Code An optional tax code associated with the category. Tax Rate The percentage of tax to apply to the purchase of associated items. Display Sequence Optionally speciﬁes the order in which tax categories will be displayed in the checkout and on receipts. Is Enabled Whether the tax category is active and applicable to ongoing sales. Apply Tax To Original Price By default, the tax rate will be applied to the ﬁnal price of an item after any discounts have been subtracted. Enabling this option will cause the tax rate to be applied to the full original price of an item regardless of any discounts applied. Creating Tax Categories Assigning Tax Categories Tax Categories

## Page 2

Tax categories can be assigned to products, POS items and subscriptions during their creation or editing process. When creating or editing a product or subscription, select the tax categories to apply from the list of available categories. See the Base Products, Point Of Sale and Subscriptions documentation for further de- tails. The reporting section of each sales business type includes a Sales Tax Report that sum- marizes the tax applied by each category over a deﬁned period. Tax Reporting Copyright © 2026 Lunchtab Inc.
