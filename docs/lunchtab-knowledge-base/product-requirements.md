Product requirements are used to indicate dietary or similar requirements on an indi- vidual basis. Examples of product requirements include vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, or any other dietary or personal requirements that may aﬀect the suitability or preparation of a product for an individual. User product requirements are utilized within the catering reports to give an indication of dietary requirements for users who have not placed an order in advance and may need a meal prepared on a given day. Product requirements for users are collected during the onboarding process and can be managed by family administrators in the My Family view of the consumer portal. System administrators can conﬁgure requirements for individual users by locating the user in the Users view and accessing the Requirements tab of the user details modal.
