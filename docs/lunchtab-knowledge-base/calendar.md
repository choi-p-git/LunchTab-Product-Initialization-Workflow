## Page 1

To conﬁgure your school year, navigate to the Calendar Settings page and select Add Calendar Year from the actions menu. You'll be presented with a form to ﬁll in the details of your school year. Give the year a name (e.g. 'School Year 2024-25') and select the start and end dates. Select the days of the week that your school is normally open (generally the days that your cafeteria will be operating). By default these are set to Mon-Fri. Exclusions for these normal days of business can be created by conﬁguring events. Once your school year is conﬁgured, you can add events that will override your normal days of business. ## Page 2

Events be a single day or a range of days. They can either close the school on days that would normally be open (e.g. holidays), or open them for business when they would normally be closed (e.g. a weekend event). To add an event, press the Events button next to the year on the Calendar Settings page to access the events and select Add Calendar Event from the actions menu. Provide a name, select the type of event (either 'Closed' or 'Open'), and select the day or range of days that the event will apply to. You can either immediately publish the event, and it will immediately aﬀect the calen- dar, or it may be left as a draft and published later. Copyright © 2026 Lunchtab Inc.
