Subscriptions allow for the purchase of a pack of products in advance, or for a discount to be applied to purchases of speciﬁc products for a limited time. The subscriptions view can be accessed by expanding the sales business type in the sidebar and selecting Subscriptions. To add a new subscription, select Add Subscription from the actions menu. Value Description Name A name for the subscription. Image An optional promotional image. Display Sequence Optionally speciﬁes the order in which subscriptions will be presented to users within the UI. Short Description A short descriptions to be shown where the subscription is listed for purchase. Long Description An optional longer description displayed in addition to the short description when viewing the detail for a subscription. Subscription Type Whether the subscription is a Plan or Pack. See Subscription Types for further details. Items Per Day Relevant to Plan subscriptions only. Deﬁnes whether the subscriber may purchase a single instance of the subscription content per-day, or an unlimited amount. Credits Relevant to Pack subscriptions only. Deﬁnes how many purchases of the subscription content can be made before the pack expires. Sales Period The period where this subscription will be available to purchase. Active Period The period where this subscription will be available to use against the purchase of products. Creating Subscriptions Subscriptions

Value Description Price The price of the subscription. Tax Categories Optional categories of sales tax to apply to sales of the subscription. See Tax Categories for further details. Discount Type The type of discount the subscription will apply. Either a ﬁxed or percentage reduction, or a replacement of the original cost. Amount The amount of discount, or the replacement amount to be applied. Availability Deﬁnes the user groups that will available to purchase the subscription, or Everyone if it should be available to all users. Content Deﬁnes the products or categories of products that the subscription is applicable to. See Subscription Content for further details. Is Published Whether the subscription is shown to users or in a hidden / draft state. A plan subscription will apply a discount to the purchase of the subscription content for the duration of the active period. The subscriber may purchase the subscription content once per-day, or an unlimited amount depending on the deﬁnition of the Items Per Day property. If the subscription is deﬁned as single Items Per Day and the subscription content con- tains multiple categories, the subscriber may purchase one item from each of those categories each day. For example, you may have a lunch plan that allows for the purchase of a single drink, meal and dessert by including those product categories. Subscription Types Plan

## Page 3

A pack is deﬁned by the number of initial Credits. A discount will be applied and the credits reduced each time the subscriber purchases one of the deﬁned content items. Products can either be included or excluded from a subscription individually, or by their associated product categories. For example, a subscription may include the Meals category, but exclude the Premium Meals category. All products assigned with the Meals category will be available for purchase with the subscription discount, except those that also have the Premium Meals category. Once a subscription has been purchased, the state of the individual subscribers can be managed by opening the actions menu of the subscription item and selecting View Subscribers. Opening the subscriber detail view will show the current state of the subscription and remaining credits / value. The subscriber detail view provides actions to temporarily Suspend, Cancel, or Refund the user's subscription. Pack Subscription Content Managing Subscribers
