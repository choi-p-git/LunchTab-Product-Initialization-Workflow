## Page 1

Cashiers can identify and manage consumers when they arrive at the point of sale us- ing the consumer panel in the checkout view. When a consumer is loaded, they will be associated with any transactions generated by the current basket when it is checked out. It is possible to check out a basket without a consumer loaded, but the account pay- ment method will be unavailable. It is generally recommended to load a consumer where possible to facilitate more accurate ﬁnancial and sales reporting. To search for a consumer, press the Next Customer Please button at the top of the con- sumer panel. This will open a search dialog where you can enter the consumer's name or other identifying information. The text input will be matched against users based on any of the following: • Name • ID • Consumer Classiﬁcation • Login Barcode • Email Address • Family Name • Family Code Once the correct user is located, select them from the list to load them into the con- sumer panel. Consumers can also be identiﬁed by presenting their registered barcode, RFID, or ﬁn- gerprint to an attached reader. Further information on managing these identiﬁcation methods can be found below. If Consumer Queue is enabled in settings, then a second consumer can be queued whilst the ﬁrst consumer is being processed. The queued consumer will be automati- cally loaded when the current consumer is unloaded. This feature is useful when setting up a single POS to handle two lines. Pressing the RFID Management button in the consumer panel will open the RFID man- agement dialog. Users can register up to two RFID cards to their account. To register a new card, press one of the empty slots and the application will wait for the card to be presented to the reader. If the card is successfully recognized, the slot will be highlighted. To remove a registered card, press the slot containing the card you wish to remove. The application will ask for conﬁrmation before removing the card. For further information on supported RFID readers, see the Supported Devices article. Orders can be added to the current basket by pressing the Add to Basket button next to each order. The order will be marked as collected when the basket is checked out and any unpaid amount will be added to the basket total. Any paid orders can be refunded by pressing the Refund button next to the order. This will cancel the order and return any paid amount to the family account that was used for payment. Pressing the Cash Account Charge button in the consumer panel will open the cash ac- count charge dialog.
