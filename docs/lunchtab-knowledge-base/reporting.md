## Page 1

Each sales business type oﬀers a number of reports focused on retail sales and sub- scription activity. We also oﬀer reports within each sales business type that are more focused on retail sales and subscription activity. See the sales business type documentation for further details. The landing page for a sales business type's reports can be accessed by expanding it in the left-hand navigation and selecting the Reporting option. The retail sales activity report provides a summary of all retail sales transactions that have occurred within the selected period. The report can be summarized by vending outlet, sales stream, or various user group- ings. Use the actions menu to Print a copy of the report, the Export Summary CSV option to export summary level information, or Export Transactions CSV to download the full transaction data. The sales item report provides a breakdown of a period's sales quantity and revenue for each product. Use the actions menu to Print a copy of the report, or the Export as CSV action to down- load the sales data. The sales category report provides a breakdown of a period's sales quantity and rev- enue for each product category. The sales category report provides a breakdown of a period's sales quantity and rev- enue for each vendor. Use the actions menu to Print a copy of the report, or the Export as CSV action to down- load the sales data. The meal ratio report indicates the proportion of users within each classiﬁcation that have purchased a meal within the selected period. Products are considered to be meals if they are assigned to a category conﬁgured with the Is Meal ﬁeld. See the Product Categories documentation for further details. Use the actions menu to Print a copy of the report. The meal sales mix report shows the quantities of each sales version of a meal product have been purchased within the selected day. Use the actions menu to Print a copy of the report, or the Export as CSV action to down- load the sales data. As a product may have more than one category applied, sales may be counted across multiple categories. This report should not be used to calculate total sales revenue. ## Page 3

The subscription activity report provides a summary of the ongoing amortization of ac- tive subscriptions, and the discounts being applied by these subscriptions to product sales. Use the actions menu to Print a copy of the report, or the Export Summary CSV option to download the summary level information. The subscriptions report shows the active subscribers for a subscription, along with the amortized + remaining values of their subscriptions. Use the actions menu to Print a copy of the report, or the Export as CSV option to download the subscriber data. The subscribers report lists all current and historic subscribers of the selected subscrip- tions, along with the current status of their subscription. Use the actions menu to Print a copy of the report, or the Export as CSV option to download the subscriber data. The daily revenue report provides a breakdown of a month's sales and subscription amortization revenue by day, with a summary of the total revenue for the selected pe- riod. Use the actions menu to Print a copy of the report, or the Export as CSV action to down- load the daily ﬁgures.
